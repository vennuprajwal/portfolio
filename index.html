About

I learn by shipping — for the people technology tends to forget.

I'm a Computer Science student at NxtWave Institute of Advanced Technologies and a full-stack developer who builds AI-enabled, voice-first products through hackathons and independent builds. I pick projects where the payoff is real — a finalist trophy, a client invoice, a repo that teaches me something I'll reuse.

Hackathons are my proving ground: TakeOver'26 finalist, HackWithHyderabad 3.0, MRDU, IIT Hyderabad and Adobe University. I can scope a build in 48 hours and still hand over something solid — that's how CyberMem, my AI incident-response agent, was born.

The work I care most about serves the last mile: trilingual, voice-first tools for kirana owners, students and everyday citizens. And cybersecurity, because tools built for vulnerable communities must also protect them.

It works for the people around me too. Teammates and clients like someone who delivers — being useful to them is how my curiosity pays off.

CyberMem

AI incident-response agent with long-term memory — it remembers how your organization solved past security incidents, and gets sharper with every one it handles.

  • Learning loop — Groq LLM reasoning coupled with Hindsight long-term memory: every incident recalls “what worked vs what failed” before recommending a response.
